Tax Basics

Understanding tax and your investments

Many financial decisions have tax implications — from how different investments are taxed to available deductions. Understanding the basics helps you plan more efficiently, though tax rules can change and often depend on individual circumstances.

This is general educational content, not personalised tax advice. Please consult a qualified tax professional for guidance specific to your situation.

What to learn

How common investments are taxed, the difference between short-term and long-term treatment, and common deductions available under prevailing tax laws.

What to learn

Estimating a retirement corpus target, understanding retirement-focused investment options, and the value of starting early.

Retirement Planning

Planning for a financially independent retirement

Retirement planning is about estimating how much you may need in the future and building towards it steadily, using a mix of long-term investment options suited to your risk appetite.

Children’s Education Planning

Planning ahead for rising education costs

Education costs tend to rise over time. Planning early, with a clear target amount and timeline, can make this long-term goal considerably more manageable.

What to learn

How to estimate future education costs, choose an appropriate investment horizon, and review the plan as your child grows.

What to learn

How to break down life goals into target amounts and timelines, and match them with suitable types of investments.

Turning goals into a plan

Whether it's a home, a wedding, or a business idea, giving each goal a target amount and a timeline makes it easier to plan and track progress.
