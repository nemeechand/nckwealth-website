Insurance Planning

Understand the role of life, health and other insurance in protecting your financial plan.

Why Insurance Matters

Protection is the foundation of a financial plan

Before focusing on growth, a sound financial plan usually starts with protecting income and assets against unexpected events — this is the role insurance plays.

Term Life Insurance

Provides a payout to your family in case of your untimely demise, at a relatively low premium, focused purely on protection.

Health Insurance

Helps cover medical expenses, protecting your savings from being used up during health emergencies.

Other Insurance

Covers such as accident or asset protection can be relevant depending on your personal circumstances.

Understand the Difference

Insurance vs investment

A common mistake is treating insurance as an investment product. Term insurance, for example, is designed purely to provide financial protection to your family — it is not designed to build wealth. Separating protection needs from investment needs generally leads to clearer, more effective financial planning.

Purpose of Insurance

To transfer financial risk — protecting your family or assets from the financial impact of unexpected events.

Purpose of Investment

To grow wealth over time by putting money into assets like mutual funds, stocks or other instruments.

Key Concepts

Terms worth understanding

Sum Assured

The guaranteed amount payable by the insurer in the event of a claim, as specified in the policy.

Premium

The amount you pay, usually annually or monthly, to keep an insurance policy active.

Riders

Optional add-ons to a base policy that provide additional, specific coverage for an extra cost.
