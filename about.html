About Nemee Chand Khichar

The founder and the purpose behind NCK WEALTH.

Founder, NCK WEALTH

Nemee Chand Khichar

Nemee Chand Khichar is the founder of NCK WEALTH, with 20 years of experience in financial awareness and investment education.

NCK WEALTH was created with a simple idea — that people make better financial decisions when they first understand the basics. Nemee's focus is on helping individuals and families understand Mutual Funds, SIP, the Stock Market, Insurance and Financial Planning, so they can approach long-term wealth creation with clarity and confidence.

NCK WEALTH brings mutual funds, SIPs, the stock market, insurance, personal finance and wealth creation into one place, explained in plain language — not as a source of guaranteed returns or one-size-fits-all advice, but as a starting point for informed, responsible financial decisions.
