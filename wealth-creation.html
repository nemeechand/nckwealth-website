Wealth Creation

Understand the principles of long-term wealth creation, compounding, diversification and disciplined investing.

The Core Idea

Wealth is usually built slowly

Long-term wealth creation tends to reward patience, consistency and time in the market — more than any single decision or product.

Compounding

Returns generated on your investment start earning their own returns over time, which can meaningfully grow your money the longer you stay invested.

Diversification

Spreading investments across asset classes helps manage the impact of any one investment performing poorly.

Discipline

Sticking to a plan through market ups and downs, rather than reacting to short-term noise.

Principles

What long-term wealth creation generally involves

Starting Early

The earlier you begin, the more time compounding has to work in your favour.

Staying Invested

Avoiding frequent withdrawals or reactive exits tends to support long-term outcomes.

Reviewing Periodically

Revisiting your goals and asset allocation from time to time, without over-monitoring day to day.
